About

Hello, I'm Arsh.

I'm a backend engineer and AI builder based in Mumbai. I spend my time writing Django APIs, designing Celery pipelines, and wiring LLMs into production systems — always backend-first, always build-to-learn.

Currently building Extractly, a multi-tenant SaaS for automated, batch CV parsing. The interesting problems: managing Celery chord orchestration across 100-file pipelines, strict Pydantic v2 validation using Gemini models and Google Vision, and secure per-tenant field isolation

Before that, I built Ghar Gift, a property management SaaS for landlords. It features comprehensive tenant management, automated WhatsApp billing alerts via Whapi, EdDSA auth, and a scalable model hierarchy (Owner → Property → Tenant) for multi-property portfolios.

I'm also deep into C++ concurrency and OS internals on the systems side, and RAG, embeddings, and fine-tuning on the AI side. Both tracks are pursued the same way: ship something real with them.

When I'm off my laptop, you'll find me in the gym, wandering with friends, or deep in a novel. And somewhere in between, falling down rabbit holes about time dilation — how time bends differently depending on gravity, velocity, where you are in the universe. The kind of thing that makes you feel small and curious at the same time.
